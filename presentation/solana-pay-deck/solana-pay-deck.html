Why it's broken

Three frictions we remove.

📝

Re-entering details

Every payer manually enters the amount and address. Errors are expensive and irreversible.

🤷

No visibility

Who paid? How much is collected? Nobody knows without manually scanning the chain.

🔗

Link-only accounting

Direct transfers to the address get lost. There's no single source of truth.

The differentiator

Direct transfers
also count.

We scan the receiver address on-chain, not the link. So even if someone pays directly from their own wallet — without ever opening your page — it still shows up in the progress.

🔍

Address-based scanning

We watch the receiver wallet, not individual links.

⚡

5-second refresh

On-chain reconciler updates progress every few seconds.

🔐

Double-spend proof

Each transaction hash is recorded once. No duplicates.
